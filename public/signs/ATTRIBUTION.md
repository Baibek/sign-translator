# Sign dictionary attribution

`public/signs/ru/` is generated by `scripts/build-slovo-dictionary.py` from the **Slovo** Russian Sign
Language dataset by SberDevices: https://github.com/ai-forever/slovo

Kapitanov A., Kvanchiani K., Nagaev A., Petrova E. *Slovo: Russian Sign Language Dataset*, 2023.

The original data is distributed under the Slovo public licence (a variant of CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://github.com/hukenovs/slovo/blob/master/license/en_us.pdf). The derived files in
`public/signs/ru/` (hand landmark sequences, one per sign) are shared under the same terms.
